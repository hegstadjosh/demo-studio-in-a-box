# Standing instructions

Copy to `local.md` beside `SKILL.md` and fill what applies. A filled heading overrides the package default; one film's choices belong in `project.md`.

**Hypothesis:** rules recorded here once spare the person repeating them. The source production loaded such rules from agent configuration each session; whether agents read `local.md` as reliably is not established. Date entries; stale ones mislead with full authority. A conflicting brief wins; note the conflict in the decision ledger.

- **Must (truthfulness):** record only what the person stated or confirmed.
- **Must (privacy):** name where credentials live, never the credentials; exclude `local.md` from shared copies.

## Review channel
Check-in destination and viewing device; whether the person usually reviews at this machine (`tools/show` opens files) or remotely (it sends them).

## Spending
Limits, approved paid services, whether helper token cost counts.

## Publishing
Where films may be posted; what needs approval first.

## Standing permissions
Allowed installs and harness hooks; consent to use the person's voice, likeness and material; which servers and watchers may outlive their work.

## Tools
Tools, services and models to prefer or avoid.

## Brand assets
Where real fonts, colours, logos and copy live.

## Compute limits
Memory, disk and time limits.
